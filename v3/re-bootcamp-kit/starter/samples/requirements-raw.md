# Raw requirements — Daily payment limit (sample input)

Source: workshop with Product and Compliance, draft 1. Not reviewed.

REQ-01 The system should let customers change their daily payment limit quickly.
REQ-02 Limits are validated.
REQ-03 If the limit is too high, the customer gets a message and/or an email.
REQ-04 The screen must be user-friendly and work on all devices.
REQ-05 The new limit is active immediately, or after approval, depending on the case.
REQ-06 Only authorized persons can approve limit changes above the threshold.
REQ-07 The system logs everything.
REQ-08 The response time should be fast.
REQ-09 Customers can raise the limit and lower the limit and see the history of changes.
REQ-10 It must be possible to block limit changes for some customers.
