# Glossary — Cards and limits

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Daily payment limit | The maximum amount a customer can pay per calendar day with one card. |
| Approval threshold | A limit amount. Changes above it need approval by a bank employee. |
| Card block | A permanent stop of a card. The customer needs a new card. |
| Card freeze | A temporary stop of a card. The customer can undo it in the app. |
| Joint account | An account with two or more account holders. |
| Identity check | A check that the person is the account holder. Done by video call or in a branch. |
